# Open Republic

**A decentralised civic network.**

Open Republic is an open-source political and policy commons.
There is no permanent leader, founder, or central authority.

Power here is **procedural**, **transparent**, and **revocable**.

---

## What This Is

Open Republic is a public space to:
- Develop political ideas and policies through open review
- Debate using structured reasoning instead of slogans
- Document decisions with version control
- Replace personality-driven politics with process-driven governance

This repository is a living system, not a finished ideology.

---

## What This Is Not

- Not a registered political party  
- Not a fan movement or follower-based organisation  
- Not a personality, ideology, or identity cult  
- Not a social media outrage machine  

Participation is based on contribution, not loyalty.

---

## Core Principles

1. **No Permanent Leaders**  
   All authority is temporary and role-based.

2. **Process Over Personality**  
   Decisions are accepted because of reasoning, not reputation.

3. **Transparency by Default**  
   All discussions, changes, and decisions are public.

4. **Forking Is Legitimate**  
   Disagreement may result in alternative versions, not suppression.

5. **Ideas Are Audited**  
   Policies must withstand critique, evidence, and revision.
